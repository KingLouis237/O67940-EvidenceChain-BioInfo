# O67940 post-2008 literature and annotation audit — interpretation

## Audit question

Has O67940 / aq_2196 acquired direct experimental functional evidence,
a revised experimentally supported identity, or an experimental structure
since the 2008 structure-guided analysis?

Audit completed: 2026-10-15. Source retrievals span 2026-10-14 to 2026-10-15.

## Main conclusion

No direct experimental biochemical characterization of O67940 / aq_2196
or a sequence-identical alias was recovered in the audited resources.

The evidence instead supports a distinction between increasingly detailed
family-level computational annotation and experimentally resolved function.

O67940 therefore remains an experimentally unresolved protein-function case
within the scope of this audit.

This is a documented negative result across defined resources, not proof
that no relevant experiment exists anywhere.

## RefSeq interpretation

The active RefSeq WP_010881445.1 record currently uses the broad product
description:

SAM hydrolase/SAM-dependent halogenase family protein.

This is family-level annotation and must not be interpreted as experimental
assignment of O67940 activity.

References attached to the RefSeq record include studies of DUF62/SAM
hydrolases and SAM-dependent halogenases in other proteins and organisms.
They provide mechanistic and family context rather than direct experimental
evidence for O67940.

The historical NP_214509.1 record remains described as hypothetical protein
aq_2196.

## UniProt annotation history

UniSave contains 102 archived O67940 entry versions spanning 1998–2026.

Across all 102 versions:

- sequenceVersion remained 1;
- no archived version contained a UniProt FUNCTION comment.

The sequence therefore remained stable while annotation changed repeatedly.

Important annotation milestones include:

- 1998: hypothetical protein;
- 2001: DUF62 family/domain annotation appears;
- 2008: putative uncharacterized protein with DUF62/PIRSF context;
- 2011: SAM hydroxide adenosyltransferase terminology appears;
- 2015: SAM chlorinase/fluorinase-related terminology appears;
- 2021: broad SAM hydrolase / SAM-dependent halogenase family statement appears;
- 2022: temporary computational recommended name "Adenosyl-chloride synthase";
- early 2023: the same specific computational name persists;
- May 2023: the specific name is removed and the entry returns to
  "Uncharacterized protein".

The transient adenosyl-chloride synthase assignment was computational rather
than biochemical evidence and was subsequently withdrawn.

## Historical interpretation

The annotation history does not show simple progressive confirmation of the
2008 SalL-like inference.

Instead, modern annotation accumulated several related but mechanistically
different labels, including:

- SAM hydroxide adenosyltransferase;
- SAM hydrolase;
- chlorinase/fluorinase-related classifications;
- broad SAM hydrolase / SAM-dependent halogenase family membership.

This indicates improved recognition of the broader structural/evolutionary
family without stable resolution of the exact biochemical reaction of
O67940.
