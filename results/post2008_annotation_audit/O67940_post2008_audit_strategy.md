# O67940 post-2008 literature and annotation audit

## Scientific question

Has O67940 / aq_2196 acquired direct experimental functional evidence,
a revised identity, a solved experimental structure, or other evidence
since the 2008 Mazumder and Vasudevan analysis that materially changes
its status as an unresolved protein-function case?

## Historical identifiers

Primary identifiers to audit:

- UniProt accession: O67940
- UniProt entry name: O67940_AQUAE
- ordered locus name: aq_2196
- organism: Aquifex aeolicus strain VF5

The audit must not assume that all later literature uses the UniProt
accession. Gene/locus identifiers, protein names, database cross-references
and sequence-linked records must also be considered.

## Evidence sources

The first programmatic audit will examine:

- current UniProtKB record;
- UniProtKB entry/history metadata where accessible;
- Europe PMC literature indexed under exact identifiers;
- RCSB PDB mappings/search results;
- current InterPro/domain annotation.

A later manual citation-following check may be used for evidence that
cannot be recovered reliably through identifier-based APIs.

## Interpretation categories

Evidence will be classified as:

1. direct experimental characterization of O67940 / aq_2196;
2. experimental evidence for a sequence-identical record;
3. experimental evidence for a close homolog only;
4. computational or transferred annotation;
5. database cross-reference without functional validation;
6. no direct evidence found.

Only categories 1 or 2 can establish experimental functional resolution
of O67940 itself.

## Negative-result rule

Failure to find evidence in one database is not evidence that no
characterization exists.

A claim that O67940 remains experimentally unresolved requires concordant
searches across multiple identifier forms and databases, with the search
date and queries preserved.

## Stop condition

This audit is complete when:

- exact identifier searches have been preserved;
- the current UniProt status and history have been reviewed;
- PDB structural mappings have been checked;
- candidate literature records have been manually classified;
- alternative identifiers found during the audit have been searched;
- the final conclusion distinguishes direct evidence, homolog evidence
  and annotation transfer.

The result may strengthen, weaken or invalidate the current premise that
O67940 remains experimentally unresolved.
